Frequently Asked Questions

Does the service include website content writing?

Yes. We organize and write content based on the information, documents, and images supplied by the client. Language and translation requirements are specified in the quotation.

Are hosting and domain fees included?

The service includes launch preparation and assistance with domain connection within the agreed scope. Domain, hosting, and third-party subscription fees will be listed separately unless explicitly included.

Is post-launch maintenance included?

Maintenance and updates are not automatically included in the initial build. They can be requested and agreed as a separate scope after launch.

Does the service include SEO?

Advanced SEO should not be assumed to be included. Basic SEO setup or additional optimization work can be defined separately in the project scope and quotation.

Can you improve an existing website instead of building a new one?

We can review the current website and recommend updates or development work after assessing its state and your requirements.
