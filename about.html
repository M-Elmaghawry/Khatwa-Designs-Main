We Understand Your Industry. We Help You Present It Better.

Founded in 2021 and headquartered in Mansoura, Egypt, Khatwa Designs is a creative and digital partner for construction companies and engineering firms. We help businesses present their expertise professionally, showcase their projects, and build a stronger market presence.

What sets Khatwa apart is our practical understanding of the engineering and construction sector. The company’s founder is an engineer who leads the work and remains directly involved in delivering selected projects. This perspective informs how we organize technical and business information and present it clearly and professionally.

We bring complementary services together under one roof, including visual identity and company profile design, presentations, tender documents and technical proposals, website design and development, and social media post design.

We have delivered projects in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. We begin by understanding each client’s needs and objectives before defining the appropriate solution. We believe design is more than appearance: it helps businesses communicate their message, demonstrate the value of their work, and present themselves in a way that reflects their business.
