Frequently Asked Questions

Do you write the content or only design the profile?

We help organize the information and write or edit the content based on the documents, information, and images supplied by the client, then complete the design and final production.

Can you update an existing company profile?

Yes. We can review an existing profile and update its content and design based on the available materials and the project objectives. The scope is confirmed after reviewing the file.

Do you independently verify technical information?

We organize, edit, and present the information clearly. The client remains responsible for the accuracy and approval of technical and business information before final delivery.

Can the profile be produced in Arabic and English?

The required languages can be agreed at the start. The quotation will specify whether content writing or translation in each language is included.

Which files will I receive?

Final formats and whether editable source files are included will be defined in the quotation before work begins.
